# Instaprenyua masterclass package

## Digital marketing masterclass

**Attract the Right Customers Online, Drive More Sales, and Keep Them Coming Back**

An eight-slide, speaker-led presentation for Tanzanian entrepreneurs using Instagram and WhatsApp. Covers organic discovery, low-cost Meta advertising, conversion, repeat business and referrals, with an illustrative TZS 35,000 advertising example.

## Scope and design status

These are newly consolidated replacements developed from the four supplied production briefs, rather than a line-by-line revision of unseen completed originals. The review report records the alignment decisions and checks.

No official brand assets were supplied. The shared white, charcoal and slate design is neutral and is not represented as official Instaprenyua branding. The illustrative businesses and situations are hypothetical; no statistics, testimonials or commercial results are invented.

External source retrieval was blocked during preparation. The narrative identifies its conceptual references and their verification limits; this package is not presented as a completed external fact-check. The toolkit link remains a clearly marked placeholder; no public toolkit URL or QR code has been invented.
